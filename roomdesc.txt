Bedroom
You wake up in a dark, abandoned bedroom with a dusty bed and broken furniture scattered 
across the floor. The windows are sealed shut, and the door is your only way out.
Something feels terribly wrong. You should look around for anything that might help you escape.
You can go EAST to enter the hallway.
#

Hallway
You step into a narrow, poorly lit hallway where flickering lights cast strange shadows along 
the walls. The air is cold, and distant noises echo through the building. Several doors
lead to unknown rooms. Move carefully, and keep an eye out for useful items.You can go WEST to
return to the bedroom, NORTH to enter the security room, or SOUTH to enter the laboratory.
#

Security Room
You enter a security room filled with dusty monitors, surveillance cameras, and old computer 
equipment. Most of the screens are dead, but one continues to flicker with a strange image.
A locked door nearby appears to lead deeper into the building. A keycard left behind on 
the desk might be useful.You can go WEST to return to the hallway 
or DOWN to enter the locked basement.
#

Laboratory
You enter a mysterious laboratory filled with shattered glass, strange chemical containers,
and abandoned scientific equipment. An unpleasant smell hangs in the air, and several 
warning signs cover the walls. A sealed chemical sample sits on a nearby table. Whatever
happened here, it was serious enough to make everyone leave in a hurry.You can go NORTH to 
return to the hallway or EAST to enter the locked basement.
#

Locked Basement
You descend into a cold, damp basement where rusty pipes run along cracked concrete walls.
The room is filled with old storage boxes and broken machinery. A heavy metal door blocks
the way forward, secured by a stubborn lock. Somewhere in the basement, a key may be the 
only thing standing between you and freedom.You can go UP to return to the security room,
WEST to return to the laboratory, or SOUTH to reach the main exit.
#

Main Exit
At last, you reach the main exit. Massive steel doors stand between you and the outside world, and a faint beam of daylight
shines through a narrow gap. After everything you have encountered, freedom seems within reach.
Find a way to open the doors and escape before it is too late.Find a way to open the doors and escape before it is too late. You can go
NORTH to return to the locked basement.
